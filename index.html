Story Shorts Uploader

A small personal tool that publishes short animated story videos to a single YouTube channel, the channel of the person who runs it.

What it does

  • Short animated videos (under 60 seconds) are created with code-based animation tools and a synthetic voice.
  • The tool uploads each finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel as private, with title, description and tags, and marks it as altered or synthetic content.
  • The owner reviews every video and decides when to make it public.
  • It can read the upload and processing status of the videos it uploaded.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to other people, has no sign-up, and does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.
